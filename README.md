# PLATINUM DESK — GTA VI Trophy Field Desk

Platinum Desk is a field desk — tracker + guide — for the 55 GTA VI trophies.

## Headline Numbers
- 55 trophies
- 337 cited field notes
- 4 sources
- 8 itinerary stages

## How the Desk Works
- **Master Table**: View all trophies at a glance.
- **Itinerary**: Follow the platinum route.
- **Watch List**: Keep an eye on trophies that can be lost.
- **Field Notes**: Detailed notes with cited sources for each trophy.
- **Filters**: Quickly filter trophies by type.
- **Spoiler Veil**: Keep trophies hidden until you're ready to see them.
- **Progress Saved**: Your progress is saved in the browser and can be exported/imported.

## How to Run It
`node serve.ts`
Then open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.

## How to Hand It In
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`
